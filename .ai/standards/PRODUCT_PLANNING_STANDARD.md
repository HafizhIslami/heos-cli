# PRODUCT_PLANNING_STANDARD.md

# Hynexis Engineering Operating System (HEOS)

Version: 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the mandatory product planning process.

Every implementation begins by understanding the product.

Never begin from technology.

Begin from business value.

---

# Product Philosophy

Software is not built to satisfy requirements.

Software is built to solve business problems.

Technology is only an implementation tool.

Every feature must create measurable value.

# Step 1

Problem Discovery

Identify:

- What problem exists?
- Who experiences the problem?
- How often?
- Why does it matter?
- What happens if nothing changes?

Never implement a solution before understanding the problem.

---

# Step 2

Business Goal

Define:

Business Objective

Expected Business Outcome

Success Metrics

Examples

Increase conversion

Reduce manual work

Reduce operational cost

Increase customer retention

Improve developer productivity

Every implementation must support at least one business objective.

---

# Step 3

Stakeholders

Identify:

Users

Administrators

Operators

Developers

Support Team

Third Party Systems

Document each stakeholder.

---

# Step 4

Functional Requirements

Define:

Inputs

Outputs

Business Rules

User Actions

Expected Responses

Dependencies

Avoid ambiguity.

---

# Step 5

Non Functional Requirements

Evaluate:

Performance

Availability

Scalability

Security

Reliability

Maintainability

Accessibility

Observability

Compliance

---

# Step 6

Constraint Analysis

Identify constraints.

Examples

Budget

Deadline

Technology

Infrastructure

Regulation

Team Skill

Legacy System

Dependencies

Never ignore constraints.

---

# Step 7

Risk Assessment

Identify:

Business Risk

Technical Risk

Security Risk

Performance Risk

Operational Risk

Migration Risk

Assign mitigation strategy.

---

# Step 8

Success Metrics

Every feature should have measurable success.

Examples

Latency

Conversion

Error Rate

Availability

Deployment Frequency

Customer Satisfaction

Revenue

Without measurable success there is no objective evaluation.

---

# Step 9

Scope Definition

Define:

In Scope

Out of Scope

Future Scope

Prevent scope creep.

---

# Step 10

Implementation Readiness

Before architecture begins verify:

□ Problem understood

□ Business objective defined

□ Stakeholders identified

□ Functional requirements complete

□ Non functional requirements defined

□ Constraints documented

□ Risks documented

□ Success metrics defined

Only then continue to architecture.

---

# Product Planning Checklist

□ Problem validated

□ Business value identified

□ Scope defined

□ Risks identified

□ Constraints identified

□ Metrics defined

□ Ready for architecture

---

# Final Principle

Great software begins with understanding.

Not implementation.
